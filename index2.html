    About This Directory

    The Global Event Centers Directory is the most comprehensive, free-to-use database of professional event venues worldwide. We list verified contact details for conference centers, exhibition halls, wedding venues, convention spaces, banquet halls and corporate event venues across Africa, North America, Europe, Asia, Oceania and South America — with strong coverage of major event hubs in Nigeria (Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt), Ghana (Accra, Kumasi), Kenya, South Africa and Egypt. Whether you're planning a corporate conference in Toronto, a trade exhibition in Frankfurt, a wedding in Mumbai or a product launch in Sydney, our directory connects event planners with the venue's official contact information — including phone numbers, email addresses, mailing addresses and official websites.
