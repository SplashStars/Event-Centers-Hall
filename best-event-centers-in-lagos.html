What to consider when choosing a venue in Lagos

  • Traffic and location. Lagos traffic can add an hour or more to a commute. Consider a venue close to where the majority of your guests are travelling from — Lekki/Ajah, Ikeja, or the Island — rather than the most impressive space across town.
  • Power reliability. Ask any shortlisted venue directly about generator backup and fuel arrangements. This affects sound, lighting, and air conditioning for the full duration of your event.
  • Parking capacity. Confirm on-site or nearby parking for your expected guest count — this is one of the most common complaints at Lagos events.
  • Capacity vs. guest list. Choose a hall sized for your actual guest count. A half-empty large hall feels flat; an overcrowded small one feels chaotic.
  • Catering policy. Some Lagos event centers require in-house catering, others allow external vendors. Confirm this before booking, as it materially affects your budget.
  • Deposit and cancellation terms. Get cancellation and rescheduling terms in writing before paying a deposit.

Popular areas for event venues in Lagos

Our directory includes venues across Lagos Mainland (Ikeja, Surulere, Yaba) and Lagos Island (Victoria Island, Lekki, Ikoyi, Ajah). Mainland venues tend to be more budget-friendly and centrally located for guests coming from across the city; Island venues often offer more premium finishes and skyline or waterfront views.
