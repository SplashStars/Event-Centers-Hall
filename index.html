    How to Choose the Right Event Center

    Picking the right venue can make or break your event. Here are the key factors every event planner should consider before booking.

    1. Define Your Guest Count

    Always book a venue that comfortably fits 10–20% more than your expected attendance. Overcrowding is a safety hazard and ruins the experience. Confirm the venue's official seated and standing capacity before signing any contract.

    2. Check Location & Accessibility

    Choose a venue that is easy to reach for most of your guests. Consider proximity to major roads, airports, hotels and public transport. In cities like Lagos or Nairobi with heavy traffic, a central location saves your guests significant travel time.

    3. Confirm Available Amenities

    A good event center should offer reliable power supply (including backup generator), high-speed internet, audio-visual equipment, parking, air conditioning and accessible restroom facilities. Ask for a detailed amenities checklist before visiting.

    4. Understand the Pricing Structure

    Ask for a full breakdown of costs: hall rental, security deposits, catering fees, setup and teardown time, overtime charges and any hidden costs. Compare at least 3 venues before making a decision. Many venues offer weekday discounts.

    5. Read Reviews & Visit in Person

    Never book a venue you haven't visited personally. Photos can be misleading. During your visit, test the acoustics, check the restrooms, inspect the parking area and speak with the venue manager about past events they have hosted. Online reviews from previous clients are also invaluable.

    6. Review the Contract Carefully

    Before signing, ensure the contract clearly states the date, time, full cost, cancellation policy, refund terms and what is included. Verbal promises don't count — everything should be in writing. Have a lawyer review contracts for large events or significant deposits.
