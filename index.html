    About This Directory

    The Global Event Centers Directory is the most comprehensive, free-to-use database of professional event venues worldwide. We list verified contact details for conference centers, exhibition halls, wedding venues, convention spaces, banquet halls and corporate event venues across Africa (Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, South Africa, Egypt), North America, Europe, Asia, Oceania and South America. Whether you're planning a corporate conference in Lagos, a wedding in Accra, a trade fair in Abuja or a product launch in Nairobi — our directory connects event planners with official contact information including phone numbers, email addresses and websites.
