About Event Center Finder

Event Center Finder is the world's most comprehensive, free-to-use directory of professional event venues. Our mission is simple: connect event planners, wedding coordinators, corporate organizers and private individuals with the right venue — anywhere in the world — quickly and without hassle.

Why We Built This

Finding the right venue for a wedding, conference, exhibition or corporate event is often harder than it should be — scattered listings, outdated contact information and paywalled directories slow planners down. Event Center Finder exists to fix that: a single, searchable, always-free directory that puts accurate venue information directly in the hands of the people who need it.
